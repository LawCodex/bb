*Livestock improperly on highways.*

**5.** (1) The Minister may, in writing, designate persons to be authorised persons for the purposes of this Act.

(2) Any livestock that is found tethered, wandering, straying, or lying on a highway or any public place may be seized by an authorised person or a constable and taken to a livestock pound where it shall be impounded until its owner claims it and pays all reasonable expenses incurred in its seizure and confinement.
