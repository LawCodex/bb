### CHAPTER 285B BARBADOS PORT INC. (TRANSFER OF MANAGEMENT AND VESTING OF ASSETS)

*2003-18.*

An Act

(a) to provide for the Port of Bridgetown to be managed by the Barbados Port Inc., a company incorporated under the Companies Act, for the purpose of enabling the Port to operate as a commercial entity;

(b) to transfer the assets and liabilities of the Government of Barbados in relation to the operation of the Port of Bridgetown to the Barbados Port Inc.;

(c) to repeal the Barbados Port Authority Act; and

(d) to provide for related matters.

[19th December, 2003] Commence-

*ment. 2003/163.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Barbados Port Inc. (Transfer of Short title. Management and Vesting of Assets) Act, 2003.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act,

"Authority" means the Barbados Port Authority established by section 3 of the former Act;

"Company" means the Barbados Port Inc., a company incorporated

under the Companies Act;

**3.** The Port shall be operated by the Company from 19th December,

*Lease of lands of Port. First Schedule.*

**4.** For the purposes of this Act, the lands of the Port which are described in the First Schedule shall be leased to the Company by the Crown for such period as the Crown determines.

*Transfer of assets and liabilities.*

**5.** (1) All

(a) assets in relation to the operation of the Port that were vested in the Authority immediately before 19th December, 2003;

(b) liabilities that were incurred by or on behalf of the Authority in relation to the operation of the Port and subsisting immediately before that date; and

(c) rights to set, collect and retain port dues and charges for the provision and use of port facilities and services

are transferred to and vested in the Company, and by virtue of this Act and without further assurance, transfer or other formality are to be held with effect from 19th December, 2003 by the Company to the same extent and interest and in the same manner as they were vested in or held by the Authority.

(2) All contracts entered into by or on behalf of the Authority in relation to the operation of the Port before 19th December, 2003 shall be deemed to have been entered into by or on behalf of the Company from that date.

(3) In respect of the operation of the Port,

(a) all rights, privileges, duties or obligations conferred or imposed upon the Authority; and

(b) all powers and remedies as to the taking and resisting of legal proceedings for the ascertaining, perfecting or enforcing of all rights or liabilities vested in the Authority

immediately before 19th December, 2003 shall be deemed to be conferred or imposed on the Company.

**6.** The Company is exempt from the payment of the property Exemption transfer tax that is payable on the transfer of the assets of the Authority from property referred to in section 5(1).

*Business plan.*

**9.** (1) The Company shall not later than 6 months after the end of each financial year submit to the Minister a business plan in respect of the ensuing financial year and for each of the 4 subsequent financial years.

(2) The business plan referred to in subsection (1) shall contain provisions for the implementation of the Port master plan and any other information that the Minister may require.

*Staff of Company.*

**10.** The officers and other employees who are members of staff of the Authority on 19th December, 2003 shall upon that date comprise the staff of the Company.

*Pension rights and pension plan.*

**11.** (1) There are hereby preserved to the staff of the Company to whom section 10 applies the pension and superannuation rights accruing to them at the time of their assumption of employment with the Company.

(2) There are hereby preserved to the officers and employees of the Authority who

(a) were members of staff of the Authority at or prior to 19th December, 2003; and

(b) have not assumed employment with the Company at that date

all pension and superannuation rights that accrued to them by virtue of their employment with the Authority.

(3) The Company shall be responsible for paying any pension or superannuation to the officers and employees referred to in subsection

(2) when the pension or superannuation accrues.

(4) Without affecting subsection (1), the service of the staff with the Authority referred to in that subsection shall be aggregated with their service with the Company and any other public service for the purpose of computing pension and other retiring benefits under this Act.

(5) Where a public officer is seconded or temporarily transferred from a pensionable office within the meaning of section 2(1) of the Pensions Act to perform any service with the Company, the service of Cap. 25. that officer shall, unless the Governor-General otherwise decides, count for pension under that Act as if the officer had not been so seconded or transferred.

(6) The Company shall, within a period of 2 years from 19th December, 2003 provide for the establishment and maintenance of a pension scheme for the benefit of officers and employees of the Company.

(7) Notwithstanding subsection (6), the Company shall for the purpose of computing pensions and other retirement benefits use the date of hire of an employee by the Company as the date for computing such pension and other benefits.

**12.** Nothing in this Act or in any regulation shall be or shall be Savings. deemed to be in derogation of any function conferred or imposed by any other law on customs officers, immigration officers, fire officers or members of the Police Force or the Defence Force, or duly authorised persons of the Ministry of Health or the Ministry of Agriculture.
