**2.** The functions, assets and liabilities of the Natural Gas Corporation established by section 3 of the Natural Gas Corporation Act are, as from 1st April, 1982, transferred to and vested in the National Petroleum Corporation established by section 3 of the NationalPetroleumAct.

*staff.*

**3.** With effect from 1st April, 1982, all persons comprising the staff of the Natural Gas Corporation shall be deemed merged with the staff of the National Petroleum Corporation, so however, that

(a) their employment in the service of the Natural Gas Corporation shall, for all purposes, be deemed to have been continuous employment in the service of the National Petroleum Corporation, and

(b) their terms and conditions of service in the employment of the National Petroleum Corporation shall be no less favourable than they were in the employment of the Natural Gas Corporation.

**4.** References in any enactment to the Natural Gas Corporation shall be construed as being a reference to the National Petroleum Corporation.
