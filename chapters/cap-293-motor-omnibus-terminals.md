*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Motor Omnibus Terminals Act.

*Interpretation .*

**2.** (1) In this Act,

“authorised person” means the General Manager of the Transport Board or any person appointed by the General Manager to perform the duties of Superintendent-in-charge of a motor omnibus terminal;

*Cap. 297.*

“General Manager” means the person for the time being holding the office of General Manager of the Transport Board established by the Transport Board Act;

“motor omnibus terminal” means any area designated under subsection (2) as a motor omnibus terminal;

“security guard” means a person appointed by and authorised by the General Manager to perform the functions of a security guard at a motor omnibus terminal and includes a senior security guard.

(2) The Minister may by order designate any area as a motor omnibus terminal.

**5.** A Security Guard shall, where a breach of this Act or the regulations has been committed, have all the powers of arrest conferred on a member of the Police Force under section 20 of the Police Act.

*Penalties.*

**6.** (1) Any person who contravenes the regulations may be removed or ejected from the motor omnibus terminal or any part thereof by an authorised person, a member of the Police Force or any other person acting on the instructions of an authorised. person and may, in addition to any penalty to which he is liable for the contravention, be deprived of the further use of any motor omnibus terminal and its facilities for such time, not exceeding 1 year, as in the opinion of the court may be necessary to ensure the safety of the motor omnibus terminal or the public.

(2) Where a person who contravenes the regulations is the holder of a permit to use any part of the motor omnibus terminal, he is liable in addition to the penalties set out in that subsection, to have his permit revoked, cancelled or suspended.

(3) Any person who enters a motor omnibus terminal or attempts to use any facility at a motor- omnibus terminal during the period for which he has been deprived of such use is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine of $1 000 or to imprisonment for 6 months or both.

*Savings.*

**7.** Nothing in this Act or in the regulations is in derogation of any functions conferred or imposed by any law on members of the Police Force or duly authorised persons of the Ministry of Transport and Works.
