### CHAPTER 8

### MINISTERS AND PARLIAMENTARY SECRETARIES (REMUNERATION AND ALLOWANCES)

*2007-22. 2008/117.*

An Act to make provision for revised salaries for 1979-5. 1991/31. Ministers and Parliamentary Secretaries. 1984-19. 1996/32. 1980/148. 1997/19. 1982/230. 1998/37. 1984/189. 1999/103. 1986-16. 2000/99. 1987-32. 2002/72. 1989/15. 2003-20. 1989/52. 2005/107.

*Commencement.*

[1st April, 1978]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Ministers and Parliamentary Secretaries (Remuneration and Allowances) Act.

*Salaries and allowances. Schedule. 1986-16.*

**2.** (1) The holder of an office specified in column 1 of the Schedule is entitled to the emoluments specified in column 2 thereof opposite that office.

(2) The holder of the office of Prime Minister may opt to receive a housing allowance at the rate specified in column 2 of the Schedule opposite that office or to occupy, free of rent, the official residence of the Prime Minister.

*Exemption from income tax. Cap. 73. 1986-16.*

**3.** Notwithstanding the provisions of the Income Tax Act,

(a) emoluments, other than salary, paid to a person in pursuance of this Act; and

(b) the housing allowance paid to the Prime Minister in pursuance of this Act or the taxable value of the official residence of the Prime Minister, as the case may be,

shall not be included in calculating the assessable income of any such person for the purposes of that Act.

*Payment after dissolution of Parliament. Schedule.*

**4.** For the purposes of this Act, emoluments payable under this Act to a person holding an office mentioned in column 1 of the Schedule shall, notwithstanding the dissolution of Parliament, continue to be payable to such person until a new appointment to that office is made.

*Power to amend Schedule. 1987-32.*

**5.** (1) The Minister responsible for Finance may, by order, amend the Schedule by increasing or decreasing any of the emoluments specified therein.

(2) An order made under subsection (1)

(a) may contain such supplementary provisions as the Minister considers necessary or expedient for the purposes of the order;

*1987-32.*

(b) may be given retrospective effect; and

(c) shall be subject to affirmative resolution.

*Payment of expenses.*

**6.** All emoluments payable under this Act shall be charged on and paid out of the Consolidated Fund in equal monthly instalments.
