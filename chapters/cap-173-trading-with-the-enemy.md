*Purchase of enemy currency.*

**8.** (1) Purchasing enemy currency shall be treated as trading with the enemy.

(2) For the purposes of this section, the expression “ enemy currency ” means any such notes or coins as circulate as currency in any area under the sovereignty of a Power with whom the Crown is at war, not being an area in the occupation of the Crown or of a Power allied with the Crown, or any such other notes or coins as are for the time being declared by an order of the Minister to be enemy currency.
