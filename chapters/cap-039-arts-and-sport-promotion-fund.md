*Accounts.*

**6.** (1) The Accountant-General shall

(a) keep proper accounts of the Fund, and proper records in relation to those accounts;

(b) prepare a statement of the Fund's accounts in respect of each financial year; and

(c) prepare a statement of the Fund's accounts in respect of each quarter.

(2) The Accountant-General shall send copies of each statement prepared by him under subsection (1)(b) to the Minister and the Auditor-General before the end of the month of August next following the financial year to which the statement relates.

(3) The Accountant-General shall send copies of each statement prepared by him under subsection (1)(c) to the Minister and the Auditor-General not later than 21 days after the end of the quarter to which the statement relates.

*Reports by Auditor-General.*

**7.** The Auditor-General shall

(a) examine, certify and prepare a report on each statement received by him under subsection (2) or (3) of section 6; and

(b) send a copy of each such report to the Minister.

*Reports to Parliament.*

**8.** The Minister shall, within 2 months after receiving a statement under subsection (2) or (3) of section 6, or a report under paragraph (b) of section 7, cause a copy to be laid before each House of Parliament.

Constitution, Procedure and Functions of the Arts and Sport Promotion Fund Committee

**1.** (1) The Committee shall comprise the Permanent Secretary, Ministry Composi-responsible for Finance, who shall be the Chairman, and 6 other members, who tion of Committee. shall be:

(c) a representative of the National Cultural Foundation;

(d) a representative of the National Sports Council;

(e) a person engaged in the arts in Barbados; and

(f) a person engaged in sport in Barbados.

(2) A reference to any person specified in paragraph (1) includes the nominee of that person where the person specified is unable to attend.

**2.** (1) The Committee shall meet at least every 3 months and at such other Meetings. times as may be necessary or expedient for the transaction of its business, and such meetings shall be held at such places and times and on such days as the Committee determines.

(2) The Chairman shall preside at all meetings of the Committee and, in the case of the absence of the Chairman, the members present and constituting a quorum may elect a Chairman from among the members present, who shall preside at the meeting.

**3.** The quorum of the Committee at any meeting is the majority of the total Quorum. membership of the Committee.

**4.** The decisions of the Committee are to be by a majority of votes and, in any Decisions. case in which the voting is equal, the Chairman or other member presiding at the meeting has, in addition to an original vote, a second or casting vote.

*Minutes.*

**5.** Minutes of each meeting are to be kept by a Secretary or other person appointed by the Committee for that purpose, and are to be confirmed by the Committee at its next meeting and signed by the Chairman or other person presiding at that meeting.

*Attendance of persons at meetings.*

**6.** (1) The Chairman may invite any person to attend a meeting of the Committee where the Committee considers it necessary to do so.

(2) A person referred to in paragraph (1) may take part in the deliberations of the Committee but shall not vote on any matter.

*Procedure. Schedule.*

**7.** Subject to the provisions of this Schedule, the Committee may regulate its own procedure.
