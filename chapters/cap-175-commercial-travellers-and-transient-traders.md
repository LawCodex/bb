*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Commercial Travellers and Transient Traders Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** For the purposes of this Act, the expression-

“ transient trader ” includes any pedlar, huckster or other person not being domiciled in this Island who carries or exposes for sale any goods, not being the growth, produce or manufacture of the Island, otherwise than in a shop or building.
