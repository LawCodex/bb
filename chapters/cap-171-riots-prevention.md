Form of Proclamation to Rioters

Our Sovereign Lady the Queen charges and commands all persons being assembled immediately to disperse themselves and peaceably to depart to their habitations or to their lawful business, upon the pains contained in the Riots (Prevention) Act of this Island. God save the Queen.
