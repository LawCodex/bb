*short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Barbados Boy Scouts Association (Incorporation) Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** For the purposes of this Act-

“ Association ” means the Barbados Boy Scouts Association;

“ National Scout Constitution ” means the body of rules adopted at any time by the Barbados Boy Scouts Association as its constitution.

**3.** The present and all future members of the Association are hereby established a body corporate to which, subject to the National Scout Constitution, section 21 of the Interpretation Act applies.
