### CHAPTER 276A

### CARIBBEAN METEOROLOGICAL COUNCIL

### (INCORPORATION)

*1968-52.*

An Act to incorporate the Caribbean Meteorological Council.

*Commencement.*

[23rd August, 1967]

*Short title.*

I. This Act may be cited as the Caribbean Meteorological Council (Incorporation) Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** For the purposes of this Act, the expression-

" Agreement" means the agreement providing for the continued operation of the Caribbean Meteorological Service established thereby, which was entered into by the govern-ments of Antigua, Barbados, British Guiana, British Honduras, the British Virgin Islands, the Cayman Islands, Dominica, Grenada, Montserrat, Saint Christopher, Nevis and Anguilla, Saint Lucia and Saint Vincent acting with the authority and consent of the government of the United Kingdom, and the governments of Jamaica and Trinidad and Tobago and which entered into force on the 2nd April, 1966 and any agreement from time to time in force in substitution therefor;

" Caribbean Meteorological Council " means the Caribbean Meteorological Council established and constituted by the ( Agreement.

*Incorporation.*

*Cap. 1.*

**3.** The Caribbean Meteorological Council shall be deemed to be a body corporate established by this Act and the pro-visions of section 21 of the Interpretation Act shall apply thereto.
