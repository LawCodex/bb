## SCHEDULE

### CHAPTER 81 PASSPORTS AND TRAVEL DOCUMENTS (FEES)

*1967-43. 1969-70. L.N.173/*

An Act to make provision for the payment of fees in respect of the issue and renewal of Barbados passports and other travel documents, and for related matters.

1967.

*1971/131. 1971/152. 1975/243. 1977/10.3 1978/124. 1980/1. 1982/39. 1982/225. 1985/25. 1985/171. 1993/65. 2005/92.*

*Commencement.*

[1st July, 1967]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Passports and Travel Documents (Fees) Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** For the purposes of this Act,

"Barbados passport" means a passport issued by or on behalf of the Government of Barbados to a citizen of Barbados;

"travel document" includes any visa, travel permit, certificate of identity or other similar document issued by or on behalf of the Government of Barbados.

*Fees for passports and other travel documents.*

**3.** (1) There shall be payable in respect of

(a) the issue and renewal of Barbados passports;

(b) the issue of travel documents; and

*Schedule.*

(c) any other matter mentioned in the Schedule

such fees as are from time to time specified in the Schedule.

*1969-30.*

(2) The Minister may waive any fee payable under subsection (1) in respect of such categories of passports and travel documents as he thinks fit.

*Variation of fees and other amendments to Schedule.*

**4.** (1) Subject to subsection (2), the Minister may from time to time by order published in the Official Gazette vary the fees specified in the Schedule or otherwise amend the Schedule in such manner as he considers appropriate.

(2) Every order made under this section shall be subject to negative resolution.

*Stamp Duty Act not to apply to passports and travel documents issued abroad. Cap. 91.*

**5.** Nothing contained in the Stamp Duty Act shall apply or be deemed to have applied to the issue abroad of any Barbados passport or travel document.
