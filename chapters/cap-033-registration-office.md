**1.** This Act may be cited as the Registration Ofice Act.

**2.** For the purposes of this Act the Registrar and Deputy Registrar shall be the Registrar and Deputy Registrar of the Supreme Court respectively.

*Powers of Deputy Registrar. cap. 117.*

**3.** Subject to the Supreme Court of Judicature Act, the Deputy Registrar may at any place or time exercise any of the powers and authorities of the Registrar, and all duties and functions for the performance and discharge of which the Registrar is required to attend the sitting of any court may be performed and discharged by the Deputy Registrar or by any clerk authorised for that purpose by the court.
