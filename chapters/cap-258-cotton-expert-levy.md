CHAPTER 258 COTTON EXPORT LEVY An Act to impose a levy upon cotton exported from the Island for the purpose offurthering th interests of the cotton industry.

*Commencement.*

**1967.** [17th June, 19351

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Cotton Export Levy Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** For the purposes of this Act, the expression-

*si lS8’*

<‘ cotton ” means clean cotton lint;

“ exporter ” includes shipper, and in the case of a steamship, the owner or agent of such vessel or exporter of cotton;

“ Minister ” means the Minister responsible for Agriculture.

*Levy on cotton exported. 1948-Z 1.*

**3.** There shall be imposed a levy upon all cotton exported from the Island, at such a rate as the Minister shall by order prescribe.

*Levytobe paid to the credit of Cotton Growers Association.*

**4.** (1) The proceeds of the levy shall on the export from the Island of the cotton be paid to the credit of the Barbados Cotton Growers Association at such bank as the Barbados Cotton Growers Association may from time to time notify.

(2) The levy shall be paid by the exporter.

*Declaration to be made by exporter.*

**5.** The exporter shall deliver to the Comptroller of Customs a declaration in duplicate containing particulars as regards weight or quantity of every shipment made by him on the form duly provided by the Comptroller of Customs for this purpose together with the receipt from the Barbados Cotton Growers Association for the payment of the levy on such shipment prior to the clearance of the ship.
