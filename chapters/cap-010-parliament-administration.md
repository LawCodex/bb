*2000/50. 2002/74. 2003/172. 2005/110. 2007/16. 2008/121.*

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Parliament (Administration) Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** For the purposes of this Act,

"Chairman" means Chairman of the Commission;

"Clerk" means the person holding the office of Clerk of Parliament;

"Commission" means the Management Commission of Parliament established under section 3;

*Fourth Schedule.*

"Constituency Assistant" means a person appointed under Part I of the Fourth Schedule;

"Deputy Clerk" means a person holding an office of Deputy Clerk of Parliament;

*1970-29.*

"former Act" means the Parliament (Debates and Establishment) Act;

"Leader of the House of Assembly" means the Minister for the time being nominated as such by the Prime Minister;

"Leader of the Senate" means the Minister for the time being nominated as such by the Prime Minister;

*ss.3-5*

"printer of debates" means the person for the time being printing the debates and proceedings of the House of Assembly and Senate;

*Second Schedule.*

"staff of Parliament" means persons who are the holders of the offices specified in the first column of the Second Schedule.

*The Management Commission of Parliament.*

**3.** (1) There is established a body to be known as the Management Commission of Parliament that shall be responsible for the administration and management of Parliament.

(2) Nothing in subsection (1) shall be construed as affecting any power conferred on the President of the Senate or the Speaker of the House of Assembly by any law in force in Barbados or any standing order of the Senate or House of Assembly, as the case may be.

*Cap. 1.*

(3) The Management Commission is a body corporate to which, subject to this Act, section 21 of the Interpretation Act applies.

*First Schedule.*

(4) The provisions of the First Schedule have effect with respect to the constitution of the Commission and otherwise in relation thereto.

*Functions of the Management Commission in respect of the staff of Parliament.*

**4.** (1) Subject to section 5, the Commission shall appoint, discipline and control the staff of Parliament.

(2) The Commission shall ensure that the grading, pay, pensions, benefits and other conditions of service with respect to the staff of Parliament, are the same as or similar to those in the public service, so far as is consistent with the requirements of Parliament.

*Delegation of functions of Commission.*

**5.** (1) The Commission may delegate to

(a) the Chairman any of its functions under this Act;

(b) the Clerk any functions other than discipline concerning the staff of Parliament.

(2) Anything done by a person to whom functions are delegated under this section in the discharge of the functions of the Commission shall have the same effect as if done by the Commission.

(Section 10(5)) P ART I

Constituency Assistants

**1.** Constituency Assistants shall be appointed in accordance with paragraph 4.

**2.** Each member of the House of Assembly shall be entitled to the services of a Constituency Assistant.

**3.** The Constituency Assistant shall be a temporary employee of Parliament.

**4.** Subject to paragraph 5, the Clerk shall, acting on the advice of the member of the House of Assembly for a constituency, appoint a Constituency Assistant for that constituency.

CAP.

**5.** The duties and responsibilities of a Constituency Assistant may be prescribed by the Clerk on the advice of the member of the House of Assembly on whose advice the Constituency Assistant was appointed.

*2008/121. code*

**6.** The Constituency Assistants shall be entitled to remuneration at the salary Z27-20 that shall be payable as follows:

$31 450.92 with effect from 1st April, 2008 $32 865.84 with effect from 1st April, 2009.

**7.** The appointment of a Constituency Assistant shall cease

(a) on one month’s notice by the Constituency Assistant, or by the Clerk acting on the advice of the member of the House of Assembly on whose advice the appointment was made or for just cause without notice;

(b) on the resignation of the Constituency Assistant;

(c) if the member of the House of Assembly who advised the appointment of the Constituency Assistant ceases to be a member of the House of Assembly other than on a dissolution of Parliament, on the day of the cessation of that membership of the House of Assembly;

(d) after a dissolution of Parliament, on nomination day if the member who advised the appointment is not nominated for election to Parliament for the same constituency; or

(e) after a dissolution of Parliament, on election day if the member who advised the appointment is nominated but is not elected to Parliament.

**8.** (1) A gratuity at the rate of one month’s salary for each year of service shall be payable to a Constituency Assistant on the completion of at least 2 years’ service, if the employment is terminated because the member of the House of Assembly who advised the appointment of the Constituency Assistant ceases for reason to be a member of the House of Assembly.

(2) For the purposes of sub-paragraph (1), “one month’s salary” means the total salary during the 24 months immediately preceding the date of termination of employment referred to in that sub-paragraph divided by twenty-four.

**9.** Any person employed as a Constituency Assistant immediately before the February, 1990 shall be treated for all purposes as if the appointment had been made as provided by this Act from the date of the appointment.
