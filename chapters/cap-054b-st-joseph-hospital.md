### THE STAFF OF THE HOSPITAL

**10.** (1) The Staff of the Hospital comprises a Hospital Administrator, the Chief of Staff and such number of doctors, nurses and other employees as the Board, subject to the approval of the Minister, may determine.

(2) The Hospital Administrator, the Chief of Staff, all other employees and all persons employed by the Board, in the performance of the duties required by them by or under this Act, shall act under the general superintendence and direction of the Board.

**11.** The Board may organise the Hospital into such divisions as it considers proper for the provision of effective care to patients.

**12.** (1) The Hospital Administrator shall be appointed by the Board with the prior approval of the Minister.

(2) The Hospital Administrator may terminate his appointment by giving three months’ notice to the Board.

(3) The Hospital Administrator shall not hold any other appointment or engage in any other occupation which in the opinion of the Board, is likely to interfere with the proper performance of his functions under this Act or is prejudicial to the interests of the Hospital.
