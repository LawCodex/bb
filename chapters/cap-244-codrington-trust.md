*1983-27.*

An Act to repatriate to Barbados the administration and control of the Codrington Estate Trust and for related matters.

*commencement. S.I. 1983 No. 144.*

[ 1 st October, 19831

Preamble

Whereas the United Society for the Propagation of the Gospel, located in the United Kingdom, is the sole trustee under the will of Christopher Codrington, who died in 1710, ofthe estate situated in the South-East corner of the parish of St. John in Barbados, which is now known as the Codrington Estate and upon a part of which stands the theological college formerly known as Codrington College and now the Provincial Theological College ofthe Province of the Church in the Province of the West Indies, hereinafter referred to as the “College”;

Whereas the United Society for the Propagation of the Gospel had heretofore delegated the performance of its executive functions under the trust of the Codrington Estate

(a) in respect of the College, to a Board of Governors of the College, and

(b) in respect of the remainder of the trust, to a Board of Management of the Estate,

under a Scheme modified and approved on the 2nd day of June, 1976, by the Supreme CourtofJudicature of Barbados and entered in the records of the High Court as Case No. 165 of 1970;

Whereas the Society now wishes to relinquish its trusteeship of the Codrington Estate to a trust established within Barbados with the agreement of the Church in the Province of the West Indies, to which plan the Church is agreeable;
