*Commencement*

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Public Officers Loan and Travelling Allowances Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** For the purposes of this Act, the expression-

“ Minister ” means the Minister responsible for Finance;

“ officer ” means a public officer who is declared by regulations made under section 4 to be eligible for the grant of loans and the payment of travelling allowances in accordance with this Act;

“ vehicle ” means motor car, motor cycle, autocycle or bicycle.

*Loans and allowances to be paid to Officers.*

**3.** (1) The Minister may direct that an officer-

(a) who makes application under this Act for a loan for the purpose of meeting the purchase cost of a vehicle, shall receive out of the Consolidated Fund by way of advance upon loan, subject to the prescribed conditions, such sum of money as the Minister determines, but not exceeding, in the case of an officer serving in Barbados, the prescribed maximum amount;

(b) shall receive out of the Consolidated Fund in respect of travelling required for the due performance of his functions an allowance at the prescribed rates and subject to the prescribed conditions.

The Minister may, if he considers it necessary, direct that an officer of such class as the Minister determines shall receive out of the Consolidated Fund by way of advance upon loans
