Functions 4. The functions of the Chamber are and objects

(a) to promote measures calculated to benefit and protect of the

Chamber.

the trading interests of its members and the general trade of Barbados;

(b) to undertake by arbitration or otherwise the settlement of disputes arising out of trade, commerce or industry;

(c) to promote and encourage the development in Barbados of manufacturing operations suitable to the community and capable of making contributions to the economic welfare and development of the country;

(d) to encourage and assist manufacturers in Barbados in the use of efftcient and modern methods of manufacture and the maintenance and proper standards of safety, labour relations, employees welfare, public relations and advertising;

(e) to promote and encourage adherence by manufacturers in Barbados to proper standards and grades of quality in all manufactured products;

v) to promote and encourage the use by consumers, by advertising trade fairs or howsoever, of products manu-factured in Barbados;

(g) to assist in securing markets for both raw materials and finished products;

(h) to consult together as to problems affecting local manu-facturing industries and to take such action as it thinks fit;

(2) All liabilities incurred by or on behalf of the former Chamber in respect of its functions under the Barbados Chamber of Commerce Act, 1868 and the rules and regulations made 1868-7. thereunder, and which by this Act are to be performed by the Chamber and subsisting immediately before 4th April, 1983 shall, as from that date, have effect as if they had been incurred by the Chamber.

(3) All books, records, furniture, equipment and other things relating thereto in the possession of the former Chamber immediately before 4th April, 1983 shall immediately be delivered to the Chamber.

(4) Any contract made before the 4th April, 1983 by or on behalf of the former Chamber in respect of the functions to be transferred to the Chamber, shall, as from that date be construed with such modifications and adaptations as may be necessary to give effect to the provisions of this section.

(5) Subject to this section, the Chamber shall have the like rights, powers, remedies (including in particular rights and powers as to taking legal proceedings) for ascertaining, perfecting or enforcing any rights or liabilities vesting in or attaching to the Chamber under this section as if they had at all times been rights and liabilities of the Chamber.

(6) All civil proceedings commenced before 4th April 1983 in any Court in Barbados by or against the former Chamber in respect of the functions to be transferred to the Chamber may be continued by or against the Chamber, and any such proceedings may be amended accordingly.
