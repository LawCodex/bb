CHAPTER 48A NATIONAL SPORTS COUNCIL An Act to provide for the establishment of an authority to facilitate the development of sport in Barbados.

[1st February, 1978]

P ART I Preliminary

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the National Sports Council Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** For the purposes of this Act

"Council" means the National Sports Council established under section 3(1);

"Fund" means the Reserve Fund established under section 8(1);

"secretary" means the person for the time being acting in the capacity of secretary to the Council.

P ART II Establishment, Status and Functions of the Council

*Establishment of National Sports Council. Cap. 1.*

**3.** (1) For the purposes of this Act, there is established a Council to be known as the National Sports Council.

(2) The Council shall be a body corporate and section 21 of the Interpretation Act shall apply thereto.

*Schedule.*

(3) The provisions of the Schedule have effect with respect to the constitution of the Council and otherwise in relation thereto.

*Functions of the Council.*

**4.** (1) The functions of the Council shall be

(a) to stimulate and facilitate the development of sport generally in Barbados;

(b) to develop, maintain and manage sporting facilities, including all playing fields and the National Stadium, provided by the Government for public use; and

(c) to administer the funds of the Council and all Government grants allotted to sporting organisations.

(2) Subject to this Act, the Council may carry on any activity that appears to it to be requisite, advantageous or convenient for, or in connection with, the discharge of its functions under this Act.

*Remuneration of members.*

**5.** A member of the Council is entitled, in respect of the office he holds, to such remuneration and allowances, if any, as the Minister determines.

P ART III Financial

*Funds and resources of the Council.*

**6.** The funds and resources of the Council shall consist of

(a) such sums as accrue from the operations of the Council;

(b) such sums or property as may become payable to or, as the case may be, vested in the Council;

(c) such sums as the Council borrows or as become available to the Council from sources other than those expressed in paragraphs (a) and (b); and

(d) such amounts as may be voted by Parliament for the purpose.

*Application of funds of Council. 1979-15.*

**7.** The funds of the Council shall be applied towards

(a) the meeting of the obligations, and the discharging of any func-tions of the Council and matters incidental to such obligations and functions; and

(b) the creation and maintenance of the Reserve Fund established under section 8.

*Reserve Fund.*

**8.** (1) The Council may, out of its funds, establish a fund to be known as the Reserve Fund.

## SCHEDULE

**1.** (1) The Council shall consist of not less than 7 persons and not more than 11 persons appointed by the Minister.

(2) The Minister shall appoint a member of the Council to be Chairman another member to be Deputy Chairman.

(3) In the case of the inability of any member to act, the Minister may appoint a person to act temporarily in the place of such member.

**2.** A member of the Council shall hold office for a term of 3 years unless he dies, resigns or has his appointment revoked before the end of that term, but

(a) a person who is appointed to fill a vacancy created by the death, resignation or removal from office of a former member shall hold office only for the unexpired portion of the term of that former member; and

(b) every member is, on the expiration of the term of his appointment, eligible for re-appointment for a further term.

**3.** The Minister may grant leave of absence to a member of the Council and may appoint a person to act temporarily in the place of that member.

**4.** A member ofthe Council, other than the Chairman, may resign his office ,by instrument in writing addressed to the Minister, transmitted through the Chairman and from the date of the receipt of such instrument by the Minister such member shall cease to be a member of the Council.

**5.** The Chairman may at any time resign his office by instrument in writing addressed to the Minister and from the date of the receipt of such

**6.** The Chairman, or, in the event of his being absent from Barbados or for any reason unable to act, the Deputy Chairman may at any time call a special meeting of the Council and shall call such a meeting within seven days after receiving a requisition to do so by any five members of the Council.

**7.** Five members of the Council shall form a quorum.

**8.** Decisions of the Council shall be by a majority of votes and where the voting is equal the Chairman shall, in addition to his original vote, have a casting vote.

**9.** The Council shall meet at such times as may be expedient for the transaction of business and such meetings shall be held at such places and times and on such days as the Council determines.

**10.** Minutes of each meeting shall be duly kept by the Secretary or other person appointed by the Council for the purpose and shall be confirmed by the Council at its next meeting and signed by the Chairman or other person presiding at that meeting.

**11.** The Council may co-opt persons to attend any of its meetings for the purpose of assisting or advising it respecting any matters with which it is dealing, but a co-opted member shall not have a right to vote.

**12.** Subject to this Schedule, the Council shall regulate its own pro-ceedings.

**13.** All documents made by, and all decisions of, the Council shall be signified under the hand of the Chairman or any member authorised to act in that behalf or by the secretary.
