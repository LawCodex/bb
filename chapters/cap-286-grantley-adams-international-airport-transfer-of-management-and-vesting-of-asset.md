## SECOND SCHEDULE

CHAPTER 286 GRANTLEY ADAMS INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT (TRANSFER OF MANAGEMENT AND VESTING OF ASSETS)

*An Act*

*2003-3. 2004/90.*

to make provision

(a) for the Grantley Adams International Airport to be managed by the Grantley Adams International Airport Inc., a limited liability company incorporated for the purpose of managing the Airport;

(b) for the effective management of the Airport by the Grantley Adams International Airport Inc.;

(c) for the transfer of the commercial assets and liabilities of the Government respecting the Grantley Adams International Airport to the Grantley Adams International Airport Inc.; and

(d) for related matters.

*Commencement. 2003/162.*

[22nd December, 2003]

*Short title.*

*Airport*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Grantley Adams International (Transfer of Management and Vesting of Assets) Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act,

"Airport" means the Grantley Adams International Airport;

*"Airport*

charge" means

(a) an amount levied on passengers in connection with their arrival at or departure from the Airport by means of an aircraft; or (b) an amount levied on an entity carrying on business within the Airport;

*Cap. 308.*

"Company" means the Grantley Adams International Airport Inc., a company incorporated under the Companies Act;

"fuel charge" means a charge paid on the gross revenue earned on the supply of fuel to an aircraft by a dealer in petroleum products;

"landing fee" means an amount levied on an operator of an aircraft in connection with the landing or parking of the aircraft at the Airport;

"parking charge" means an amount levied on the driver of a motor vehicle in connection with the parking of the vehicle at the Airport.

*Management of Airport.*

**3.** Subject to this Act, the Company is hereby authorised to manage and maintain the Airport.

*Lease of lands of Airport. First Schedule.*

**4.** For the purposes of this Act, the lands of the Airport which are described in the First Schedule shall be leased to the Company by the Government for such period as the Government determines.

*Transfer of assets etc.*

**5.** (1) Subject to section 4, the

*Second Schedule.*

(a) assets in relation to the Airport that are identified in the manner described in the Second Schedule and were owned by the Government immediately before 22nd December, 2003;

(b) commercial liabilities that were incurred by or on behalf of the Government and were subsisting immediately before 22nd December, 2003; and

(c) rights to set, collect and retain Airport charges, landing fees, fuel charges, parking charges or any other commercial charge or fee in connection with the provision and use of Airport facilities and services

*s.6*

are transferred to and vested in the Company, and by virtue of this Act and without further assurance, transfer or other formality are to be held with effect from 22nd December, 2003 by the Company to the same extent and interest and in the same manner as they were vested in or held by the Government.

(2) All contracts entered into by or on behalf of the Government in respect of the Airport before 22nd December, 2003 shall, as from that date, be deemed to have been entered into by or on behalf of the Company.

(3) In respect of the commercial operation of the Airport,

(a) all rights, privileges, duties or obligations conferred or imposed upon the Government; and

(b) all powers and remedies as to the taking and resisting of legal proceedings for the ascertaining, perfecting or enforcing of all rights or liabilities vested in the Government

immediately before 22nd December, 2003 shall be deemed to be conferred or imposed on the Company.

*Annual report and financial statements.*

**6.** (1) The Company shall within 3 months of the end of each financial year submit to the Minister the report, the audited financial statements, and the auditor's report referred to in subsection (2).

(2) The Minister shall as soon as possible after the end of each financial year and not later than 6 months after the end of that year lay in Parliament

(a) a report of the operations of the Company during the preceding year, including a comparison of the performance of the Company with the corresponding business plan; and

(b) audited financial statements for the preceding year consisting of

(i) statements of profit, loss and cash flow;

(ii) such other statements as may be necessary to show the financial position of the Company and the financial results of the Company's operations during that financial year, including any contingent liabilities of the Company; and

(iii) an auditor's report on the financial statements.

*Airport master plan.*

**7.** (1) The Company shall once in every 10 years submit an Airport master plan to the Minister for approval.

(2) The Airport master plan referred to in subsection (1) shall contain

(a) the details of the objectives, strategies and policies of the Company;

(b) a marketing plan;

(c) a land use and environmental plan; and

(d) any other relevant information which may be required by the Minister.

*Business plan.*

**8.** (1) The Company shall no later than 6 months after the end of each financial year submit to the Minister a business plan in respect of the ensuing financial year and for each of the subsequent 4 financial years.

(2) The business plan referred to in subsection (1) shall contain provisions for the implementation of the Airport master plan and any other information that the Minister may require.

*Pension plan. Cap. 25.*

**9.** (1) Any public officer who was employed in a pensionable office within the meaning of the Pensions Act and who accepts employment with the Company shall retain all rights in respect of pension, gratuity or other like benefit accruing to that public officer immediately before 22nd December, 2003, and those rights shall be preserved.

(2) The Company shall within a period of 3 years from 22nd December, 2003 provide for the establishment and maintenance of a pension fund for the benefit of the employees of the Company.

(3) The Company shall, for the purposes of computing pensions and other retirement benefits to be paid out of the pension fund referred to in subsection (2), use the date of hire of an employee by the Company as the date for computing such pensions and benefits.

*Applicability of Cap. 288A and Cap. 285A.*

**10.** As from 22nd December 2003, the Company is subject to the provisions of

(a) the Civil Aviation Act, except section 10; and

(b) the Airports Act, except section 4.

*Savings.*

**11.** Nothing in this Act or in any regulations shall be deemed to be in derogation of any functions conferred or imposed by any other law on customs officers, immigration officers, fire officers, members of the Police Force or duly authorised persons of the Ministry of Health or the Ministry of Agriculture.

*Amendment of Schedules.*

**12.** The Minister may by order amend the Schedules.
