### CHAPTER 2A

*1970-46*

!'"' An Act to provide for the amendment or revocation of Orders-in-Council.

*Commencement.*

[31st December, 1970]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Orders-in-Council (Amendment and Revocation) Act.

*Inter-. pretataon.*

**2.** For the purposes of this Act-

• •

" Order-m-Council " means an Order-in-Council made under a United Kingdom Act;

"Order-in-Council to which this Act applies" means an Order-in-Council which applies, or applies the provisions of any United Kingdom Act with or without modification, to Barbados, but excludes-

( a) the Barbados Independence Order, 1966, and the Schedule thereto;

(b) any Order-in-Council which applies to Barbados by virtue of any Act of the Parliament of Barbados.

*Amendme~t ~f revocation g~~~cii~; ~~ J?rime m1Ster.*

**3.** ( 1) The Prime Minister may by order amend or revoke any Order-in-Council to which this Act applies.

(2) An order made under subsection ( 1) may contain such consequential, supplemental or ancillary provisions as are necessary and expedient for giving due effect to the order, and accordingly an order which amends an Order-in-Council to which this Act applies may, where it appears to the Prime Minister necessary or expedient to do so, modify any provision of a United Kingdom Act which has been applied to Barbados by that Order-in-Council.

(3) An order made under subsection (1) shall be subject to negative resolution.
