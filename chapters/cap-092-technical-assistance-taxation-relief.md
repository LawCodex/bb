*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Technical Assistance (Taxation Relief) Act.

*Exemption from certain taxes of certain employees of the Govem-ment of the U.S.A. cap. 177. Cap. 179. cap. 285. Cap. 295.*

**2.** Notwithstanding anything in-

(a) the Dog Licences Act; or

(b) the Firearms Act; or

(c) the Wireless Telegraphy Act; or

(d) the Road Traffic Act,

no tax or fee shall be payable by any employee of the Government of the United States of America assigned to duties in this Island in connection with technical assistance programmes or projects to be carried out wholly or in part in Guyana, British Honduras or any present or former British West Indian Island, under the provisions of the Agreement for Technical Co-operation entered into between the Government of the United Kingdom and the Government of the United States of America at London on the 13th July, 1951, in respect of any licence issued to him under any of the said Acts or in respect of any motor vehicle of which he is the owner:

Provided that such employee is-
