**1.** This Act may be cited as the Limitation of Remedies (Scient$c and Research Projects> Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** For the purposes of this Act, the expression

“actions” includes any civil proceedings howsoever commenced;

“court” includes any court or judge having the jurisdiction, power or authority to grant an injunction:

“injunction” means any interim or perpetual injunction.

*~~;~~;~,*

**3.** (1) Notwithstanding any provision or rule of law to the contrary, no court shall, in any proceedings to which this Act applies

(a) grant any injunction; or

(b) make any declaratory order or judgment; or

(c) grant any other relief or remedy save damages.

*Schedule.*

(2) The proceedings to which this Act applies are actions (whether or not commenced before the 16th December, 1965’) against any person in respect of any act or omission, or any threatened or apprehended act or omission, of any person in the course of the execution of any scientific or research project set out in the Schedule.
