*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Public Library Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** For the purposes of this Act, the expression-

“ the public library ” means the library and museum called the Barbados Public Librarv and Museum and shall be deemed to include the branch library in Speightstown maintained under this Act;

“ trustees ” means the persons for the time being appointed under this Act to be trustees.

*E;F:; and museum.*

**3.** (1) The public library shall be open and free to all persons residing in Barbados, subject to any rules and regulations which may be made by the trustees under section 5.

(2) The library shall consist of such books, prints, maps, philosophical and other instruments and apparatus and such natural and scientific objects and productions of art as are from time to time contributed thereto by donation and accepted by the trustees or purchased out of any moneys which may be contributed for that purpose by private persons or otherwise or out of any sums which may be voted for the purpose by Parliament.

(3) The Governor-General shall be the patron of the public library.

*Trustees authorised to dispose of useless books.*

**9.** The trustees are hereby authorised to sell or otherwise dispose of all books, pamphlets and other publications belonging to the public library, which are or may hereafter become useless or not worth repairing, and to pay the proceeds of any such sale to the Accountant-General to be paid into the Consolidated Fund.

*Grant for the maintenance of a public library in Speights-town.*

**10.** (1) An annual grant of the sum of one hundred and seventy-two dollars and eighty cents in four equal quarterly instalments, shall be paid to the trustees for the maintenance in Speightstown of a library.

(2) The said library in Speightstown shall form part of and be a branch of the public library.

**11.** All sums payable under this Act shall be charged upon and paid from the Consolidated Fund, and unless herein otherwise directed shall be payable in equal monthly sums.
