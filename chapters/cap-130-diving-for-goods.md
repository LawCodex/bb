### CHAPTER

### DIVING FOR GOODS

*1919-6. 1960-29.*

An Act to prohibit the diving for goods and merchandise in Carlisle Bay and the Careenage by unlicensed persons.

*Commencement.*

[lst May, 19191

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Diving for Goods Act.

*No unlicensed person to dive for %Z$F Careenage.*

**2.** (1) No person shall dive for goods or merchandise in Carlisle Bay or the Careenage unless he is the holder of a licence granted by the Comptroller of Customs permitting him to do so.

(2) A licence shall be issued under subsection (1) only on a certificate from the Commissioner of Police that the applicant is a fit and proper person to hold a licence and shall be in force for one year from the date of issue unless it be revoked by the Comptroller before the expiration of the year.

(3) This enactment shall not apply to any person diving for any article immediately after such article has fallen into the water, at the request of the owner, consignee or agent of such article or of the person superintending the loading or unloading thereof.

**3.** Any goods or merchandise dived up in Carlisle Bay or the Careenage shall be handed over to the Comptroller of Customs, who shall deliver the same to the person who in his opinion is the owner thereof, on such person paying to the person who dived for such article the sum agreed on between the owner and the diver or, in default of such agreement, the sum fixed by the Comptroller.

*Penalty. 196S29.*

**4.** Any person violating any of the provisions of this Act shall be liable on summary conviction, on the information of the Port Manager, any employee of the Port Department authorised in that behalf by the Port Manager or any member of the Police Force, to a penalty of ten dollars.
