CHAPTER 380B NATIONAL CULTURAL FOUNDATION An Act to provide for the establishment of a cultural foundation for Barbados.

*Commencement. 1984/15.*

[30th January, 1984] Citation

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the National Cultural Foundation Act.

### P ART I

Establishment, Status and Functions of the Foundation

*Establishment of Foundation.*

**2.** (1) The National Cultural Foundation, in this Act referred to as the "Foundation", is established by this Act.

*Cap. 1.*

(2) The Foundation is a body corporate and section 21 of the Interpretation Act applies thereto.

*Schedule.*

(3) The Schedule has effect with respect to the constitution of the Foundation and otherwise in relation thereto.

*Functions of*

**3.** (1) The functions of the Foundation are

(a) to stimulate and facilitate the development of culture generally;

(b) to develop, maintain and manage theatres and other cultural facilities and equipment provided by the Government;

(c) to organise cultural festivals; and

(d) to do anything necessary or desirable to assist persons interested in developing cultural expression.

(2) Subject to this Act, the Foundation may carry on any activity that appears to it to be requisite, advantageous or convenient for or in connection with the discharge of its functions under this Act.

(3) The Foundation may establish agencies to assist it in carrying out its functions under this Act.

*Remuneration of members.*

**4.** A member of the Foundation is entitled, in respect of the office he holds, to such remuneration and allowances, if any, as the Minister determines.

**4A.** Where a public officer who has pensionable service is transferred to or becomes employed in the service of the Foundation, his service with the Foundation shall, whether or not there was a break in service, be aggregated with his service in the public service and his pension shall be calculated in accordance with the Pensions Act and the Pensions Regulations, 1947 as if all of his service was service in the public service.

P ART II Financial

*Funds and resources of the Foundation.*

**5.** The funds and resources of the Foundation consist of

(a) moneys accruing from the operations of the Foundation;

(b) moneys or property payable to or, as the case may be, vested in the Foundation;

(c) moneys borrowed by the Foundation or that become available to the Foundation from sources other than those mentioned in paragraphs (a) and (b); and

(d) moneys voted by Parliament for the purpose.

*Application of funds of*

**6.** The Funds of the Foundation shall be applied towards

(a) the payment of the salaries, wages, fees, allowances, pensions and gratuities of persons employed or formerly employed by the Foundation;

(b) the meeting of the obligations, and the discharging of the functions of the Foundation and matters incidental to such obligations and functions; and

(c) the creation and maintenance of the Reserve Fund established under section 7.

## SCHEDULE (Section 2(3))

**1.** (1) The Foundation consists of not less than 7 persons and not more than 11 persons appointed by the Minister.

(2) The Minister shall appoint a member of the Foundation to be Chairman and another member to be Deputy Chairman.

(3) In the case of the inability of any member to act, the Minister may appoint a person to act temporarily in the place of such member.

**2.** A member of the Foundation holds office for a tenn of 3 years unless he dies, resigns or has his appointment revoked before the end of that term, but

/a) a person who is appointed to till a vacancy created by the death, resignation or removal from office of a former member holds office only for the unexpired portion of the term of the former member; and

(b) every member is, on the expiration of the term of his appointment, eligible for re-appointment for a further term.

**3.** The Minister may grant leave of absence to a member of the Foundation and may appoint a person to act temporarily in the place of that member.

**4.** A member of the Foundation, other than the Chairman may resign his office by instrument in writing addressed to the Minister, transmitted through the Chairman and from the date of the receipt of the instrument by the Minister the member ceases to be a member of the Foundation.

**5.** The Chairman may at any time resign his office by instrument in writing addressed to the Minister and from the date of the receipt of the instrument by the Minister the Chairman ceases to be Chairman, and if the instrument so specifies, ceases to be a member of the Foundation.

**6.** The Chairman or, in the event of his being absent from Barbados or for any reason unable to act, the Deputy Chairman may at any time call a special meeting of the Foundation and shah call such a meeting within 7 days after receiving a requisition to do so by any 5 members of the Foundation.

**8.** Decisions of the Foundation are by a majority of votes and where the voting is equal the Chairman has, in addition to his original vote, a casting vote.

**9.** The Foundation shall meet at such places and times for the transaction of business as the Foundation determines.

**10.** Minutes of each meeting shall be duly kept by the Secretary or other person appointed by the Foundation for the purpose, and shah be confirmed by the Foundation at its next meeting and signed by the Chairman or other person presiding at that meeting.

**11.** The Foundation may co-opt persons to attend any of its meetings for the purpose of assisting or advising it respecting any matters with which it is dealing, but a co-opted member does not have a right to vote.

**12.** Subject to this Schedule, the Foundation shah regulate its own proceedings.

**13.** All documents made by, and all decisions of, the Foundation shah be signified under the hand of the Chairman or any member authorised to act in that behalf or by the Secretary.
