CHAPTER 211 STATUTE OF FRAUDS An Act for prevention of frauds and perjuries.

[ 16th February, 17621

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Statute of Frauds.

*No act+ ~~~~~~f unless $$ to ’ ‘g65-35.*

**2.** No action shall be brought whereby to charge the defendant upon any special promise to answer for the debt, default or miscarriages of another person, or upon any contract or sale of lands, tenements or hereditaments or any interest in or concerning them, unless the agreement upon which such action is brought, or some memorandum or note thereof, is in writing and signed by the party to be charged therewith or some other person thereunto by him lawfully authorised.
