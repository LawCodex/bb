*short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Employment Exchanges Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** For the purposes of this Act, the expression-

“ employment exchange ” means any office or place established under section 3;

“ Minister ” means the Minister responsible for Labour.

**3.** The Minister may-

(a) establish and maintain, in such places as he thinks fit, employment exchanges, that is to say, offices or places for the collection and furnishing of information, either by the keeping of registers or otherwise, respecting persons who seek to engage employees either in this Island or elsewhere and persons who seek employment; (b) make grants, or advances by way of loan, out of such funds as may be provided by Parliament, to meet the expenses of persons desiring to travel to places outside of the Island where work is available for them, upon such terms and conditions as he may determine.

*Regulations.*

**4.** (1) The Minister may make regulations for all or any of the following purposes, that is to say-

(a) to regulate the management of employment exchanges ; (b) to prescribe the forms and records to be used or kept by exchanges ;
