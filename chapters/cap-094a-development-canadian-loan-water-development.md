An Act to authorise the Government to borrow from the Government of Canada for the purposes set out hereunder a sum of money not exceeding the amount so set out and for matters incidental thereto and connected therewith.

*Commencement.*

[24th August, 19701

*short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Development (Canadian Loan) (Water Development) Act.

**2.** Notwithstanding the provisions of any other enactment, the Government is hereby authorised to borrow from the Government of Canada a sum of money not exceeding $10 000 000 in the currency of Canada on such terms and conditions as may be agreed between the Government and the Government of Canada.

*Appropriation.*

**3.** The money borrowed under the authority of this Act shall be appropriated and applied to finance expenditure to be incurred in the purchase of machinery, services, materials and equipment in connection with the development of the water resources and water supply of Barbados.

*Char@%.*

**4.** The payment of the principal money borrowed under the authority of this Act and of any interest thereon or other charges or expenses connected therewith is hereby charged on the general revenues and assets of Barbados.
