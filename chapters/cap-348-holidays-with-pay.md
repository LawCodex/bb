**6.** (1) Where any employee has been employed by an employer for a period of not less than three months’ continuous employment and such employment is terminated, the employer shall forthwith pay to the employee in addition to all other amounts due to him, his average pay for the period of his employment with such employer:

Provided that if the employee has at any time taken an annual holiday under this Act while in the employment of that employer, such employer shall be deemed to have complied with the provisions of this section if he pays forthwith to the employee, in addition to all other amounts due to him, his average pay for the period of his employment between the date on which he became entitled to his last annual holiday and the date of the termination of his employment.

(2) For the purposes of this section, the expression “ three months’ continuous employment “, in relation to an employee other than an employee employed on a weekly, fortnightly, monthly or yearly basis, means employment for an aggregate of forty-eight days in three consecutive months of the year of employment.
