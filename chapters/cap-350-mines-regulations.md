*Lowering or raising of persons.*

**12.** A single linked chain shall not be used for lowering or raising persons in any working shaft or plane.

*Machinery to be fenced.*

**13.** All exposed and dangerous parts of the machinery used in or about the mine shall be and shall be kept securely fenced.

*No tamper-ing with safety devices.*

**14.** No person shall wilfully damage or without proper authority remove or render useless any fence, fencing, casing, lining, means of signalling, signal, cover, chain or appliance or thing provided in any mine in compliance with this Act or any rules.

*Observance of directions.*

**15.** Every person shall observe such directions with respect to working as may be given to him with a view to complying with this Act or any rules.
