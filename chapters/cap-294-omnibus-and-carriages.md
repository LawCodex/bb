**11.** (1) No person shall act as a driver of an omnibus, livery Driver to be

carriage or hackney carriage until he has obtained a licence from the Commissioner of Police authorising him to do so, and any person acting in contravention of this subsection shall be liable to a penalty of two dollars for every day during which he acts as such driver.

(2) Any person desiring to obtain a licence to drive an omnibus, livery carriage or hackney carriage shall apply to the Commissioner of Police who shall grant such licence if he is satisfied that the applicant is of good character, competent and not suffering from any loathsome, infectious or contagious disease.

(3) The licence granted to any driver by the Commissioner of Police shall be made terminable on the fifteenth day of January in the year next succeeding the year in which it was First issued, and shall be in the form in the First Schedule. Schedule.

(4) Where any licensed driver is convicted of a third or subsequent offence under this Act, the magistrate may, in addition to any other punishment which he may impose, order the licence to be forfeited.

(5) The Commissioner of Police shall keep a record of all licensed drivers, and of the convictions of such drivers for breaches of the Laws of this Island.

(6) The clerk of every magistrate shall forward to the Commissioner of Police a copy of every conviction of a licensed driver and the Commissioner of Police shall furnish such clerks with the names of licensed drivers.

**13.** (1) The driver of every omnibus, livery or hackney carriage wherein any property is left by any person shall, within twenty-four hours, carry such property, if not sooner claimed by the owner thereof, in the state in which he finds the same, to the police station of the district in which he resides and shall there deposit and leave the same with the officer on duty.

(2) Every such driver acting in contravention of subsection (1) shall be liable to a penalty of forty-eight dollars.

(3) The officer with whom any such property is deposited shall forthwith enter in a book to be kept for that purpose the description of such property and name and address of the driver who brought the same and the number of his carriage and the day on which it is brought.

(4) The property so entered shall be returned to the person who proves to the satisfaction of the Commissioner of Police that the same belonged to him, such person previously paying all expenses incurred, together with such reasonable sum to the driver who brought the same, not exceeding five per cent of the estimated value, as the Commissioner of Police awards.

(5) Where such property is not claimed by and proved to belong to some person within one month after the same has been so deposited, the Commissioner of Police shall cause such property to be sold or otherwise disposed of and the proceeds thereof to be paid over to the Consolidated Fund, all expenses incurred about such property, together with such reasonable sum to the driver who brought the same as the Commissioner of Police awards, being first paid therefrom.
