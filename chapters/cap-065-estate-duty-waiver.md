### CHAPTER 65

### ESTATE DUTY (WAIVER)

An Act to provide for a waiver of estate and succession duties.

1998-49. (24th December, 1998) Commencement.

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Estate Duty (Waiver) Act.

Short title.

**2.** (1) No estate duty or succession duty under the Estate Duty Waiver of Act, 1895 is payable in respect of the estate of a person whose death estate duties. occurred on or before 31st March, 1981.

Act 1895-4.

(2) Subsection (1) shall not operate to affect or invalidate any estate duty or succession duty paid before 24th December, 1998.
