### CHAPTER 233B

### NATIONAL HOUSING CORPORATION (VESTING OF PROPERTY)

*1975-57.*

An Act to transfer to and vest in the National Housing Corporation established by the Housing Act, certain lands now vested in the Crown.

[8th January, 19761

*short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the National Housing Corporation (Vesting of Property) Act.

*:;Fpreta-cap. 226.*

**2.** For the purposes of this Act, “ the Corporation ” means the National Housing Corporation established by the Housing Act.

**3.** All the land described in Part 1 of the Schedule and which immediately before the commencement of this Act was vested in the Crown is by this Act and without further assurance, as from the date of such commencement, transferred to and vested in the Corporation for an estate in fee simple subject to the encumbrances described in Part II of the Schedule, but free from all other encumbrances whatsoever.

## PART II

Encumbrances affecting the lands of the Crown by this Act vested in the Corporation

The land vested by this Act in the Corporation is subject only to the following encumbrances-

(a) the restrictive covenants contained in an Indenture of Conveyance dated the 26th day of October, 1945, recorded in the Registration Office in Volume 625 of Deeds at Folio 128 and made between Joseph Evan Webster of the One Part and Radio Distribution (Barbados) Limited of the other Part; and

(b) certain rights of way over certain roads, at the commencement of this Act, constructed thereon.
