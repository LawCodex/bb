*Preamble.*

[27th September, 19351 WHEREAS it is expedient to define in one Act the terms and conditions applicable to loans hereafter authorised to be raised by the Government;

AND WHEREAS it is expedient to provide for the creation of stock and to enable the Island to take advantage of the Colonial Stock Act, 1877, of the United Kingdom Parliament, and the subsequent Acts on the same subject.l

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the General Loan and Stock Act.

*Interpretation. 1955-26.*
