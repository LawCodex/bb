Preliminary

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Trespass to Property (Reform) Act.

*Definitions.*

**2.** In this Act,

“occupier” includes

(a) a person who is in physical possession of premises; or

(b) a person who has responsibility for and control over the condition of premises or the activities there carried on, or control over persons allowed to enter the premises, notwithstanding there is more than 1 occupier of the same premises;

“premises” means land and structures, or either of them, and includes

(a) ships and vessels;

(b) trailers and portable structures designed or used for residence, business or shelter; and

(c) motor vehicles and aircraft, except while in motion.
