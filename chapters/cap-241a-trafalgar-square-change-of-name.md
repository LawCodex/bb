### CHAPTER 241A TRAFALGAR SQUARE (CHANGE OF NAME)

An Act to change the name of the area known as the Trafalgar Square. 1999-5. [26th April, 1999] Commencement.

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Trafalgar Square (Change of Name) Short title. Act.

**2.** As from 28th April, 1999 the place in the City of Bridgetown National theretofore known as Trafalgar Square shall be named "National Heroes Square. Heroes Square".
