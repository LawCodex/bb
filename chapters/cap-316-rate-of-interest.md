*Short title:*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Rate OfInterestAct.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** For the purposes of this Act, the expression

“exempted person” means any person or class of persons exempted by .order under section 6 from the provisions of section 3,4 or 5;

“judgment” means a judgment, order or decree of the High Court;

“Minister” means the Minister responsible for Finance;

“prescribed rate” means the rate of interest prescribed by the Minister by order under section 6 as the highest rate chargeable in respect of any contract, transaction or judgment, as the case may be, by any person or class of persons specified in any such order.

*Maximum rate of interest on contracts\**

\-3. (1) No person, other than an exempted person, may in any contract made in Barbados after 8th October, 1973.

(a) directly or indirectly charge, secure or reserve a rate of interest higher than the prescribed rate; or

(b) charge with, or make liable for, interest at a rate higher than the prescribed rate, any real estate in Barbados.

(2) Any contract made in contravention of subsection (1) shall be void and may not be sued on or enforced in any court in Barbados or by any process of any such court.
