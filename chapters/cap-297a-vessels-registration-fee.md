### CHAPTER 297A

### VESSELS (REGISTRATION FEE)

*1982-x.*

An Act to provide for the impositiop of a registration fee on operators of certain vessels in respect ofthose vessels.

*Commencement.*

[ 1st January, 19831

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Vessels (Registration Fee) Act.

*Definitions,*

**2.** In this Act,

*Cap. 295.*

“Licensing Authority” has the meaning assigned to it by section 2 of the Road Trafic Act;

“operator” means an operator of a vessel that provides passenger service, cruise service or pleasure trips starting and ending in Barbados;

“vessels” means any launch, boat, ship or sailing vessel.

**3.** Every operator shall register his vessel with the Licensing Authority and at the time of registration pay to the Licensing Authority such fee as the Minister may prescribe.

**4.** (1) Every operator who registers his vessel in accordance with section 3 shall, in the month of January in every subsequent year after registration, pay such annual registration fee as the Minister may prescribe.

(2) An operator who is required to pay the annual registration fee under this section and fails to pay the fee within the time specified in subsection (1) shall, in respect of his registration fee for that year, pay to the Licensing Authority, in addition to the fee, a sum equal to the fee.

**5.** The Licensing Authority must keep a register in which he must enter the following particulars in respect of any vessel required to be registered under section 3:
