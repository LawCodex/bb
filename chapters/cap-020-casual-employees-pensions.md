*Grant of pensions and gratuities.*

**3.** (1) The Minister may, subject to the provisions of this Act, grant pensions and gratuities to casual employees who have been in the public service.

*Pensions and gratuities not as of right.*

**5.** An employee shall not have an absolute right to compensation for past services or to any pension, gratuity, or other allowance; nor shall anything in this Act prevent an employee from being dismissed at any time and without compensation.

**14.** (1) A pension or gratuity granted under this Act shall not be assignable or transferable except for the purpose of satisfying

(a) a debt due to the Crown; or

(b) an order of any court for the periodical payment of sums of money towards the maintenance of the wife or former wife or minor child of the employee to whom the pension or gratuity has been granted.
